# ExampleCo Widgets: product overview (source notes)

Synthetic fixture for the Helmet Write walkthrough. ExampleCo is a fictional
company; every detail below is invented.

## Audience for the deck

Operations managers at ExampleCo customers who have not yet moved to Widgets 4.

## Points to cover

1. What Widgets is: an order tool for small warehouses.
2. Why move to 4.x: offline mode (4.0) and shared order lists (4.1).
3. What it costs to move: no price change; existing orders carry over.

## Figures

- Median order screen load time fell from 2.6 seconds to 1.1 seconds between
  Q2 and Q3, measured on the 30 September production sample.
- 1,510 active customers at the end of Q3.

## Order flow (for the diagram)

An order is built online or offline. Offline orders wait in a device queue.
Either way the order reaches the ExampleCo order service, which confirms or
rejects it.

## Not in scope

- Pricing tiers, discounts and any Q4 date other than the planned November 4.2
  release.
