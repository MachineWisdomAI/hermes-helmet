# ExampleCo Widgets: what shipped in Q3 (source notes)

Synthetic fixture for the Helmet Write walkthrough. ExampleCo is a fictional
company; every figure below is invented.

## Releases

- Widgets 4.0 shipped on 14 July. It adds offline mode and a new order screen.
- Widgets 4.1 shipped on 9 September. It adds shared order lists for teams.
- Both releases were announced to customers by email on the day they shipped.

## Quarter figures

| Measure | Q2 | Q3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active customers | 1,240 | 1,510 |
| Orders placed through Widgets | 18,400 | 26,900 |
| Median order screen load time (seconds) | 2.6 | 1.1 |
| Support tickets per 100 customers | 9.5 | 7.2 |

## Notes from the product team

- The load-time drop comes from the new order screen in 4.0. It was measured on
  the 30 September production sample, not in the lab.
- Offline mode queues orders on the device and sends them when the connection
  returns. Orders placed offline are counted in the order total once sent.
