# Example writing guidance

A neutral, adopter-replaceable style pack for writing cards. Load it, then read
`references/house-style.md` for the details. It sets a floor. A card's own
audience, purpose, sources and instructions always win where they differ.

## Everyone

- Write for the audience and purpose named on the card. If either is missing,
  ask your coordinator through the card instead of guessing.
- Use only the sources the card lists. A claim without a listed source is a
  question for the coordinator, not a gap to fill from memory.
- Plain, specific, active prose. Define a term once, then keep using it.
- Numbers keep their units and their source. Do not round away a figure that a
  reader will compare.

## Writers

- Produce your assigned part only. Do not rewrite another part or the final
  document.
- Match the format the card names: a document section, or a slide group with
  speaker notes. Keep speaker notes to what the presenter says, not a copy of
  the slide.
- When changes are requested, address each point and say what changed. Do not
  carry in material from other cards.

## Editor

- Assemble from the accepted inputs the card lists, and nothing else.
- Keep each writer's facts and figures intact. Make the voice consistent, the
  order logical and the transitions clean.
- Cut repetition. Do not add new claims. Flag a contradiction between inputs to
  the coordinator instead of resolving it silently.

## Diagrams

When a diagram helps, author Mermaid and render it with the `diagram` skill.
Embed the PNG and keep the `.mmd` and `.svg` as separate files.

This pack is an example. Replace it with your own through the company-skill
importer rather than editing it in place inside a running worker.
