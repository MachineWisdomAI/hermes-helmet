# Example house style

This reference belongs to the neutral `example-writing-guidance` pack.

## Audience and voice

- State the audience in the first paragraph of a document or on the title slide
  notes. Write at the level of a busy, intelligent reader who is new to the
  topic.
- Voice: direct, calm, specific. Prefer "we recommend" over "it is suggested".
- Avoid filler openers, hedging stacks, and marketing superlatives.

## Structure

- Documents: a one-paragraph summary, then sections in the order the plan lists.
  Each section opens with its main point.
- Decks: one idea per slide. A title that states the point, at most five short
  bullets, and speaker notes that carry the explanation.
- Tables: a header row, one fact per cell, units in the header.

## Sources and figures

- Cite the card's listed sources by name where a claim depends on them.
- Quote a figure exactly as the source gives it. Mark an estimate as an
  estimate.

## Review

- A revision answers each requested change in order. Say "done" or explain why
  not.
- The editor's final document reads as one voice, with every accepted input
  represented.
