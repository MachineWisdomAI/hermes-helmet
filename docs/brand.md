# Hermes Helmet and Machine Wisdom AI

Hermes Helmet is an open-source software factory by [Machine Wisdom AI](https://machine-wisdom.ai/).

## Name and attribution

Use **Hermes Helmet** in full in prose. Keep `helmet` for the CLI and preserve actual technical identifiers.

Use **By Machine Wisdom AI** for article and creative bylines, and **Hermes Helmet by Machine Wisdom AI** when the product and its maker share one line. Link the company name or logo to [machine-wisdom.ai](https://machine-wisdom.ai/). Keep the product's primary invitation focused on the quickstart.

The exact tagline is:

> Give Hermes the wings to carry your plan through, under its own identity

The signature emoji is **🪽**. Use it sparingly where it fits the composition; it need not sit to the left of every name. It supplements the product emblem and company logo.

Company attribution does not replace factual credit. Preserve Nous Research's credit for Hermes Agent and the recorded contributions of workers, reviewers, and other contributors. Attribute personal experiences to the person who had them; do not turn a founder's story into an invented company “we.”

Pair it with the Machine Wisdom AI logo and a readable company attribution on websites, covers, social graphics, and press material. Use a secondary position near the byline or footer so the product message remains clear.
